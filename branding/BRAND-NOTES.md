# Wiremoth logo concept

**Design:** A geometric moth whose angular wings resemble diverging signal paths, with antennae terminating in connection points. Copper/orange and charcoal give the security-tool feeling of Burp Suite without borrowing Burp's bolt or logo construction.

**Primary palette**
- Midnight: `#141A22`
- Signal orange: `#FF7542`
- Ember: `#FF9250`
- Ivory: `#F7F3EC`

**Files**
- `wiremoth-symbol.svg`: transparent, scalable standalone mark
- `wiremoth-icon.svg`: standalone mark in rounded midnight tile (app icon)
- `wiremoth-logo-dark.svg` and `wiremoth-logo-light.svg`: full branded lockups
- `wiremoth-wordmark-transparent.svg`: wide logo on transparent background for dark UI
- `wiremoth-icon-512.png`, `wiremoth-icon-128.png`, `wiremoth-icon-32.png`: raster app icons
- `wiremoth-logo-dark.png`, `wiremoth-logo-light.png`: ready-made PNGs
- `wiremoth-brand-board.png`: preview and palette

These SVGs use live text with a system-font fallback (`Inter`). For critical print usage, convert the text to vector outlines using your preferred vector editor. No fonts are bundled.

This is a first creative concept, not a legal trademark or distinctiveness clearance.
