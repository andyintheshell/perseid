# Project Governance

## Governance model

Perseid is a founder-led open-source project maintained by [@andyintheshell](https://github.com/andyintheshell).

The Project Lead has final authority over the project's technical direction, roadmap, architecture, contribution acceptance, releases, official branding, and governance.

Community feedback, bug reports, and pull requests are welcome and valued, but decisions are not made by voting or consensus. The Project Lead may accept, modify, defer, or decline proposals at their discretion, taking into account project goals, maintainability, security, and available review capacity.

## Contributions and reviews

All contributions to the official repository require the Project Lead's approval before merging. External contributors and reviewers do not receive merge or release authority by default.

Technical review or discussion does not imply a commitment to merge a contribution. Substantial changes should be discussed in an issue before implementation.

## Roles

- **Project Lead:** @andyintheshell. Maintains the roadmap, reviews contributions, makes final decisions, manages releases, and administers the official repository.
- **Contributors:** Community members submitting code, documentation, issues, or feedback.
- **Reviewers:** Individuals who may be invited to provide technical reviews without acquiring independent decision-making authority.
- **Continuity Custodian:** A separately designated trusted person who may exercise limited emergency authority under the project's succession policy.

## Decision-making and disagreements

Technical disagreements should be discussed respectfully in the relevant issue or pull request. The Project Lead resolves disagreements and makes the final decision.

## Continuity

The project maintains a separate continuity and succession arrangement for cases where the Project Lead becomes permanently unavailable.

Emergency authority is not part of normal project governance. Its purpose is to preserve the project and enable maintenance if the Project Lead can no longer perform that role.
